Recommended: an open feed

Questions sit on one shared surface with subtle separators. There’s less framing around each item, while the question stays the first thing you read.

  • A short, welcoming introduction and one clear “Ask a question” action.
  • Compact sorting and filtering, with pull-to-refresh retained in the actual app.
  • A full question with a quiet UTC timestamp. The count/upvote control sits alongside it. Touch targets remain at least 44 px.
  • Counts stay readable when voting is unavailable. Your own question is marked; the upvote control is disabled with an accessible explanation.
  • After an upvote, a brief confirmation explains when the allowance resets. Moderator-only removal keeps its reason/confirm/cancel flow.
  • Translations and accessibility text sizes can require more lines even with a short character limit. Show the complete question rather than an ellipsis or a smaller font.

Proposed copy and visual treatment, not a production screen. The current app/database limit remains 280 characters. No new limit is selected by this prototype. All dates and counts here are illustrative.
