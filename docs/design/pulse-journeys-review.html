Review baseline and limits

Guests browse/share and may cast the daily YES/NO vote. Verified sign-in is required for Brewing submissions/upvotes. No blanket login-day ban is recommended; the comparison is an optional policy under review. A CAPTCHA is not proof of a physical device. Real authorization, uniqueness, attestation, session replacement, one-time import and moderation require server implementation.

Preferences here use browser local storage only; they do not prove account/database synchronization. Role switches are design controls, not authentication. The current app and its 280-character limit remain unchanged. Proposed new-question limit: 180 grapheme clusters, subject to approval and matched server enforcement.
