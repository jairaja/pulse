# [Feature] Brewing submission/upvote journeys, daily rules and discoverable views

Guests browse/share; verified members submit one question and upvote one other person's question per UTC day. Keep self-upvote and global daily allowance checks on the server. A conditional login wait is a separate reason, not an already-used vote.
