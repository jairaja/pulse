# [Technical] Canonical account history and account/device separation through logout

Update the prior account lifecycle issue with first-signup-only import and explicit consent decisions. An existing account restores its own canonical history, never automatically imports a new device's guest answers. History links and device anti-abuse relationships are separate. New guest votes after logout must remain unowned instead of being credited to the last bound installation account. Clearing private caches does not erase authoritative quotas; do not expose an earlier account's choices through guest receipt recovery.

Cover many accounts on a shared device, one account on many devices over time, import decline, lost-response replay, deletion/recovery, immutable totals and minimum private association retention. Keep already accepted votes authoritative and avoid a generic two-way overwrite. See points 4–5,10,26.
