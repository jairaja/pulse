# [Security] Risk-based CAPTCHA assurance without physical-device or lifetime-session claims

CAPTCHA is bot friction, not proof of physical hardware, identity or a unique human. Provider tokens are short-lived/single-use and cannot authorize unlimited later actions. Design a separate server-issued scoped/expiring assurance signal if reducing repeated puzzles, plus fresh request-bound integrity checks according to platform protocol.

Step up on new/suspicious sessions/devices, replay/velocity/risk or assurance expiry; do not remove current per-vote CAPTCHA until an equivalent reviewed control ships. Gate the write after verification, cover provider outage/accessibility/false positives, and do not challenge public browsing/sharing. Link T17 and T10. See points 13–14.
