# [Feature] Useful My Pulse for guests and canonical member activity

Guest My Pulse shows only accepted unowned answers on this device, clearly local and separate from any signed-out account; honest empty state and signup/import invitation. Members see daily answers, dated upvotes, submitted and selected questions, with links to exact public states. Selection is a status of a submission, not an extra submission; repeated daily upvotes are not unique supporters.

Use accessible text-first summaries, time/activity bars and precisely defined majority alignment among the user's finalized answered questions. Exclude ties/zero/unfinalized data from the declared alignment denominator, show sample count and avoid personality/population claims. Preserve unavailable-content states without leaking private audit reasons. Test logout/account-switch privacy and empty/low-volume/chart alternatives. See points 3,18.
